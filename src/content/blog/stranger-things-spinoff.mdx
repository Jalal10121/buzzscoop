The Duffer Brothers are returning to Hawkins — but not the way you think. Netflix has officially greenlit a **Stranger Things spin-off series** set in the same universe.

## What's It About?

Sources say the spin-off will explore the **origin of the Upside Down** and introduce an entirely new cast of characters. It's being described as "a prequel with a twist."

## Production Timeline

- **Writing:** Already underway
- **Filming:** Expected to begin early 2027
- **Release:** Late 2027 or early 2028

## Will Original Cast Return?

The Duffer Brothers have hinted that **one major character** from the original series will appear in a surprising capacity.
